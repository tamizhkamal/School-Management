Welcome to the Super 30 Model Schools

Our mission is to empower Tamil Nadu’s brightest students from government schools with world-class NEET coaching. Through the Super 30 initiative, we select top students from each district and provide them with dedicated support, resources, and expert guidance. Our goal is to help them excel in the NEET exam and secure a bright future in the medical field.

Join us in building a healthier tomorrow, one student at a time.
